01The brand

Sweden, in a bottle.

Still and sparkling water from Sweden, made for the moments that matter.

HAVA began with a simple idea: water this pure deserves a bottle as beautiful as the place it comes from. Heavy glass, a brushed gold cap and four letters, drawn as clean as the Swedish landscape.

Sweden is a land of ancient granite, deep pine forest and around 100,000 lakes. Rain and snowmelt sink slowly through the stone and come back up cold and clear.

  • Pure

    Clear water, clear glass, clear words. Nothing added that doesn’t need to be there.

  • Calm

    Space, slow light and quiet confidence. HAVA never shouts.

  • Swedish

    Lakes, forest, stone and ice. The landscape is in everything we show.

  • Made for the moment

    Long dinners, slow mornings, the aperitif and everything in between.

02Logo

The wordmark

  • Four letters, one line weight

    HAVA is drawn in clean, even strokes. The A is a peak with no crossbar; the V is the same peak turned over.

  • The line belongs to the logo

    The line under the letters runs their full width. Keep it in every version except the plain wordmark.

  • Never retype it

    The logo is drawn, not typed. Always use the supplied files (SVG, PNG or PDF) and never rebuild it from a font.

10Voice

How HAVA speaks

Calm, short and concrete. Like the landscape: nothing extra. We describe what is real (the water, the glass, the place) and let it speak for itself.

Do
  • Short sentences. One idea each.
  • Concrete words: lake, stone, glass, gold.
  • Facts with a source (e.g. Yale EPI 2024).
  • Talk to the guest: “your table”.
Don’t
  • Shout, exclaim or use capitals for emphasis.
  • Claims we can’t prove (“the purest in the world”).
  • Health or medical promises.
  • Jargon and buzzwords.
