About HAVA

What is HAVA?

HAVA is still and sparkling water from Sweden. It comes in heavy clear glass bottles with a brushed gold cap, in a light bottle for the day, and as sparkling flavours in 330 ml cans.

Where does HAVA come from?

From Sweden, a land of ancient granite, deep pine forest and nearly 100,000 lakes. You can read more on The source page.

The products

Which sizes are there?

HAVA Still comes in 1000 ml and 700 ml glass and a 500 ml bottle. HAVA Sparkling comes in 700 ml glass. The sparkling flavours come in 330 ml cans.

Which flavours are there?

Nine: lime, strawberry, blueberry, raspberry, watermelon, apple, peach, orange and mixed berries.

What is in the flavours?

Sparkling HAVA with natural fruit flavours and no preservatives.

How should HAVA be stored?

In a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight. Serve it chilled.

Can the bottles and cans be recycled?

Yes. Glass bottles, plastic bottles and cans can all be recycled. Please follow your local recycling rules.
