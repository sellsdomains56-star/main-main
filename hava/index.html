Källan · The source

From Swedish ground

Sweden has about 100,000 lakes, long cold winters and some of the oldest bedrock in Europe. Rain and snowmelt sink slowly through sand, gravel and granite, and come out the other side cold and clear.

That slow trip through the ground is the whole recipe. We bottle Hava in Sweden and leave the water the way we found it.

Vattnet · The water

What's in the bottle

  • Rent

    Pure

    Rain and snowmelt filter slowly through Swedish sand, gravel and granite. By the time we bottle it, the ground has done the cleaning.

  • Kallt

    Cold

    Best at 6–8 °C. Keep it in the fridge, or sink it in a lake for an hour.

  • Klart

    Clear

    Low in minerals and soft on the tongue, with nothing added.
