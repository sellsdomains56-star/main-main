Checks that matter for any island off-plan

  • Freehold status and the exact plot/title type for the unit.
  • Developer track record: handovers on time, past delays.
  • Escrow account registered with the regulator; payments only to it.
  • Infrastructure timeline: bridge/road access, utilities, retail, schools.
  • Supply pipeline vs. rental demand on the island.
  • Service charge vs. comparable communities.
  • Exit liquidity: resale transactions in similar units.
