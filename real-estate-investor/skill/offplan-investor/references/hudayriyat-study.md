# Al Hudayriyat Island: investment study (draft)
_Prepared with the Abdullah Ahli Off-Plan Investor skill._
Data: ADX Interact snapshot, 12 Oct 2026 (`data/hudayriyat-adxinteract-2026-10-12.json`). Not independently verified. Not financial advice.

## What the data says
- **Market is entirely off-plan:** 3,451 sales, AED 28.85B, 100% off-plan. Exits before handover depend on resale demand from later buyers; there is no ready-unit market to benchmark.
- **Median AED 1,901/sq ft overall.** Small units cost more per sq ft: 1-bed 2,340, 2-bed 2,250, versus 1,337 for 6+ bed. Check the unit you are offered against its own bedroom band, not the island median.
- **Volume is concentrated:** the top 10 projects are most of the sales; several are new supply in 2026 (Golf Estates, Bashayer phases). Heavy new supply raises resale competition.
- **Plots distort the average.** Wadeem plots (418/sq ft) are not comparable with built units.

## Why it could be good
- Strong buyer activity in 2026 (AED 28.85B) shows demand at launch prices.
- Compact units price at the island's highest per sq ft, which suggests buyers pay for entry-level product.

## Why it could be bad
- No rental yield on the page. Without rent data, you cannot confirm income; the calculator's net-yield check cannot be run on market evidence.
- 100% off-plan: no secondary market reference.
- Heavy 2026 supply of similar product.
- The published +324.6% price change is probably a mix effect, so do not use it as a growth rate. Use 0 to 4% in the simulator and test 0%.

## Data gaps to fill before investing
Rental comparables, resale transactions of handed-over units, developer delivery record, service charges, infrastructure timeline, yearly price series.
