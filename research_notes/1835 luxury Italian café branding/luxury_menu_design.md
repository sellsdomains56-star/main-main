# Luxury Italian café menu design and content (for "1835", Dubai)

Research date: 23 November 2026. Method note: where I say "PDF read directly", I downloaded the venue's own PDF and extracted its text, page size and embedded fonts (pdftotext / pdfinfo / pdffonts). Where I say "search snippet only", the venue's site blocked access (HTTP 403/404) and the fact comes from a search-result summary that I could not verify against the page. Menus I could **not** open: Marchesi 1824, Cova, Caffè Florian, Gilli, Sant'Eustachio, Bulgari Hotels (all PDFs 403), Rocco Forte / Stravinskij Bar (403), Armani Hotel Dubai (404), Four Seasons Firenze (403).

## 1. Layout: formats, sizes, grid, white space, section order and titling

### Takeaway
The luxury Italian menus I could read directly are small-format booklets (A5 at Prada Caffè) or deliberately "editorial" objects (a tabloid newspaper at Gran Caffè Quadri, an A3 "news" sheet at Caffè Pedrocchi), with one topic per page, centred all-caps section titles, service hours under the title, and legal text confined to a small footer. Section order follows the day: breakfast, pastry, gelato, savoury, lunch, soft drinks, coffee, cocktails, wine.

### Cited Findings
- **Prada Caffè (Harrods) menu, PDF read directly:** 20 pages, page size 419.5 x 595.3 pt = 148 x 210 mm (A5 portrait); PDF created 20 June 2025. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- Prada Caffè page order: (p.3) a full-page welcome text about the space, materials and tableware; BREAKFAST "9AM – 11:30AM"; PASTRY TRADITION "ALL DAY" (three pages: croissants/pralines/marron glacé, then "Small cakes – Modern Classic", then "Small cakes – Italian Heritage"); GELATO AND MILKSHAKES; SAVOURY SELECTION "ALL DAY"; LUNCH "12PM – 9PM" (two pages); DRINKS; COFFEE AND HOT DRINKS; COCKTAILS (Classic, then Signature + No Alcohol); WINES (four pages). Each page carries one section title, centred, in capitals, with the service hours centred beneath it and a centred page number at the foot. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- Prada Caffè keeps few items per page: e.g. the breakfast page has 5 mains + 5 "adds"; each small-cakes page has 5–6 items; the classic cocktail page has 6 drinks. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- Prada Caffè opens with brand narrative rather than product: the welcome page describes the green walls, the black-and-white chequered floor copied from the 1913 Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II boutique, "pale-blue porcelain inspired by ancient Celadon pottery", blown crystal and silver-plated cutlery. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- **Gran Caffè Quadri (Piazza San Marco, Alajmo family) piazza menu, PDF read directly:** 12 pages at 807.9 x 1218.9 pt = about 285 x 430 mm, laid out as a newspaper ("Numero 02") with a front-page index ("MENU … pag 5", "BREAKFAST … pag 3", "VINO … pag 7"), a HISTORY feature and a QR-linked documentary. Text is fully bilingual, Italian first then English, in multi-column grids. — [Quadri piazza menu PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0508/7356/5353/files/Menu_Piazza_Quadri_24_-_review_-_SINGOLE_d5773d5d-d9ed-43e8-8618-6cd8e0b05a23.pdf)
- **Caffè Pedrocchi (Padua, historic café) pastry/caffetteria list, PDF read directly:** a single A3 sheet (841.9 x 1190.6 pt) titled "Pedrocchi News N.31", arranged in newspaper-style columns with boxed sections: HEALTHY, CAFFETTERIA PEDROCCHI, OUR TEA WITH BISCUITS, OUR HOT CHOCOLATE, PASTICCERIA, SNACKS… ANY TIME. — [Pedrocchi menu PDF](https://caffepedrocchi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/past_en.pdf)
- **Belmond Hotel Cipriani, Bar Gabbiano cocktail list, PDF read directly:** 15 pages at 623.6 x 841.9 pt = 220 x 297 mm; opens with the hotel's story (Giuseppe Cipriani, opened 1958), then "The Famous Signature Cocktails of Hotel Cipriani", "Mocktails", then wines by the glass. States "100% recycled paper". — [Bar Gabbiano menu PDF](https://pdfs.belmond.com/cip-gabbianobar-cocktails-eng.pdf)
- Luxury hospitality menus are commonly held in rigid covers: Masterfold's Buddha-Bar Paris covers use 3 mm board inside hard covers with gold metal corners and a hot-stamped gold foil logo (search snippet only). — [Masterfold](https://masterfold.com/?p=25022)
- Unedo boutique hotel (Designmono): spirits, wine and food menus have natural leather covers branded by pyrography, with digitally printed interior pages; snack and cocktail menus are separate cards (search snippet only). — [Designmono, Unedo](https://oleander-carnation-g9bx.squarespace.com/projects/unedo-boutique-hotel-visual-identity-printing)
- Hotel Collegio alla Querce, Florence (Auberge; art direction Saint-Lazare, production Atelier Bulk): menus and menu holders for restaurants and bars, with custom rigid supports padded in green fabric from Manifattura del Seveso. — [Atelier Bulk](https://www.atelierbulk.com/en/?p=6948)

### Inferences
- Two valid models emerge: (a) the "jewel-box booklet" (Prada: A5, one category per page, lots of air), and (b) the "house gazette" (Quadri, Pedrocchi: large sheet, dense, bilingual, storytelling). For a new brand that wants to read as expensive rather than historic, (a) is the safer reference; (b) only works when there is real history to print.
- A sensible 1835 structure borrowing Prada's sequence: welcome/story page; Caffetteria; Cioccolata; Tè e infusi; Pasticceria (cornetti, mignon, monoporzioni/torte); Salato (tramezzini, toast, pizzette); Gelato; Bevande; Aperitivo analcolico. This order is my recommendation, not something a source prescribes.
- Service hours under the section title (Prada) are a cheap, elegant way to handle day-parts without separate menus.

### Gaps
- No source gave margins, grid or column measurements; nothing I read quantifies white space. Prada's density (5–6 items per A5 page) is the only hard proxy.
- I could not open the physical/PDF menus of Marchesi 1824, Cova, Florian, Gilli, Sant'Eustachio, Bulgari, Stravinskij Bar, Armani or Four Seasons Firenze, so their formats and bindings are unverified.
- Art of the Menu: the Caffè Storico (NY, by de Vicq design) entry does not describe format, type or production; I found no Art of the Menu entry for a luxury Italian café that documents paper or finishes. — [Art of the Menu, Caffè Storico](https://underconsideration.com/artofthemenu/archives/caff_storico.php)

## 2. Typography conventions and how descriptions are written

### Takeaway
Real menus are more varied than the "Bodoni + script" cliché: Pedrocchi does use Bauer Bodoni, but Prada uses its own brand typeface with a plain sans, Quadri uses Albertus with Futura, and Bar Gabbiano uses Playfair Display with Garamond italic. The shared conventions are capitals with open letterspacing for item names or section heads, a quiet secondary face for descriptions, and descriptions that are one line of ingredients with provenance, not adjectives.

### Cited Findings
- **Fonts embedded in the Prada Caffè PDF:** "PradaTypeface" (a proprietary face), Athelas Italic, and Arial (regular, italic, bold, bold italic). — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- **Fonts embedded in the Pedrocchi PDF:** Bauer Bodoni Std Roman and Century (including a small-caps cut, "Century-SC700"). — [Pedrocchi menu PDF](https://caffepedrocchi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/past_en.pdf)
- **Fonts embedded in the Quadri PDF:** Albertus Medium and Futura (Light, Book, Medium, obliques). Section heads are letterspaced capitals ("B R E A K F A S T", "C I C C H E T T I"). — [Quadri piazza menu PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0508/7356/5353/files/Menu_Piazza_Quadri_24_-_review_-_SINGOLE_d5773d5d-d9ed-43e8-8618-6cd8e0b05a23.pdf)
- **Fonts embedded in the Bar Gabbiano PDF:** Playfair Display (regular, italic, bold) and EB Garamond Italic. Drink names are widely letterspaced capitals ("B E L L I N I", "N E G R O N I  D I  L A G U N A"). — [Bar Gabbiano menu PDF](https://pdfs.belmond.com/cip-gabbianobar-cocktails-eng.pdf)
- **Prada description style (verbatim examples):** "Eggs Florentine — Poached Cacklebean eggs, toasted brioche, spinach, hollandaise and Parmesan aged 24 months"; "Negroni — Campari, vermouth, gin"; "Spritz Aperol — Aperol, prosecco, soda". Item name in sentence case on one line, ingredient list on the next. Named provenance does the selling: "Faroe Island smoked salmon", "San Daniele prosciutto", "Bronte pistachio cream", "Ivy House butter", "12 years balsamic vinegar DOP". — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- Prada glosses sub-categories in lower case after a capitalised label: "CROISSANTS oven-baked sweet laminated dough", "PIZZETTA baked puffed dough", "TOASTS toasted brioche bread". — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- **Language mix at Prada (London):** English throughout, with Italian kept for proper names only: "Lemon Delizia", "Tiramisù", "Pizzetta", "Crostoni", "Chickpeas Panelle", "Chinotto Lurisia", and wines listed by Italian denomination. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- **Language mix at Quadri (Venice):** item name in Italian capitals, English gloss beneath in a lighter weight: "CAFFÈ ESPRESSO SHAKERATO / Cold espresso shaken with ice"; "LATTE MACCHIATO / Steamed milk stained with coffee"; "LÌ PER LÌ / Mini cappuccino from Trieste". Bilingual sub-heads use a dash: "Tè e Infusi - Teas and Herbal Teas", "Acque Minerali - Mineral Water". — [Quadri piazza menu PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0508/7356/5353/files/Menu_Piazza_Quadri_24_-_review_-_SINGOLE_d5773d5d-d9ed-43e8-8618-6cd8e0b05a23.pdf)
- **Bar Gabbiano description pattern:** three tiers per drink: letterspaced name; ingredient line ("Prosecco DOCG, Saint Germain, violet, lavender, lime"); one short italic tasting/story line ("Signature drink inspired by Serenissima perfumers"). — [Bar Gabbiano menu PDF](https://pdfs.belmond.com/cip-gabbianobar-cocktails-eng.pdf)
- General (blog-level) guidance: menu body text of roughly 10–12 pt or more because dining rooms are dim; avoid hairline weights for body text because thin strokes vanish in low light; keep high-contrast Didone-style faces (e.g. Playfair Display) for names and section titles; proof under the room's actual lighting. — [Made Good Designs](https://madegooddesigns.com/best-fonts-for-menus/)

### Inferences
- The consistent system across all four PDFs is two voices: a display voice for names/heads (capitals, tracked out) and a subordinate voice for descriptions (italic serif or light sans). A script face appears in none of them.
- "No adjectives" holds for Prada; Quadri and Gabbiano allow a small amount of wit or story. For 1835, ingredient-plus-origin lines (e.g. "Bronte pistachio", "Piedmont IGP hazelnut") are the proven register.
- For Dubai, the Prada pattern (English body, Italian only for names) is the closest precedent for an international audience; the Quadri pattern (Italian caps + English gloss) is more theatrical and uses more space, which matters once Arabic is added.

### Gaps
- No authoritative typographic source on small caps, tracking values or point sizes for luxury menus was found; the only numeric guidance is from a design blog.
- I could not determine which embedded font Prada uses for which level (the PDF text extraction does not expose that), nor actual point sizes in any menu.
- Typefaces of Marchesi, Cova, Florian, Gilli, Bulgari, Armani menus are unverified (menus not accessible).

## 3. Pricing presentation and menu psychology

### Takeaway
The one robust academic finding is Cornell's: plain numerals without a currency sign produced higher spend than "$" or spelled-out prices. Real luxury menus mostly follow that (Prada, Bar Gabbiano), but famous Italian cafés break the "rules" freely: Quadri prints "€8.00" with dotted leaders and Pedrocchi prints "6 ,00" with leaders.

### Cited Findings
- **Cornell study:** Yang, Kimes and Sessarego, "$ or Dollars: Effects of Menu-price Formats on Restaurant Checks" (Cornell Center for Hospitality Research, issued 1 May 2009; also in International Journal of Hospitality Management 28:1). Lunch checks at St. Andrew's, the Culinary Institute of America restaurant in Hyde Park, NY, with three formats: "$20", "20", and "twenty dollars". Guests given numeral-only menus spent significantly more than the other two groups; the authors caution it may apply only to lunch at that restaurant. — [Cornell eCommons record](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/entities/publication/1ca7d603-ee0c-4721-9003-91ca4f87cfa2)
- The Cornell Chronicle reports the effect as an average of $5.55, about 8%, more per diner with no dollar signs, from 201 diners; no difference between "$" numerals and spelled-out prices; Kimes attributes it to currency references triggering the "pain of paying". — [Cornell Chronicle, 9 Dec 2009](https://news.cornell.edu/node/273666)
- A search summary of another listing of this work states price presentation "was not found to be a significant predictor" relative to other factors; the eCommons abstract reconciles this: format mattered less than party size or time at table but still produced significant differences between formats. — [Cornell eCommons record](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/entities/publication/1ca7d603-ee0c-4721-9003-91ca4f87cfa2)
- **Prada Caffè price format:** a single "£" printed once as a column header at the top right of each page; prices right-aligned in a column with no leader dots and no symbol; whole numbers where possible ("13", "15", "18") and unpadded decimals otherwise ("12.5", "17.5", "5.75", never "12.50"). Category-wide prices are stated once against the heading ("TEA AND HERBAL INFUSION SELECTION 6.5", "MILKSHAKES 15", "SOFT DRINKS 5.25"). — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- Prada uses uniform price points within a section: every croissant 11.5, every praline 3.5, every small cake 17.5, every classic cocktail 18, every signature cocktail 20, every no-alcohol cocktail 17. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- Prada coffee prices (2025 PDF): espresso 5.75, americano 6.75, cappuccino 7.5, flat white 6.75, latte 6.75, hot chocolate 10. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- **Bar Gabbiano price format:** the number follows the drink name on the same line with no symbol, dots or decimals ("B E L L I N I 30"); a single line at the front reads "Prices in euros, service included - VAT included". Wines show glass price after the name and "120 Bottle" in the description line. — [Bar Gabbiano menu PDF](https://pdfs.belmond.com/cip-gabbianobar-cocktails-eng.pdf)
- **Quadri price format (counter-example):** euro sign, two decimals and spaced dotted leaders: "CAFFÈ ESPRESSO . . . . €8.00", "CAPPUCCINO . . . €13.00", "CAFFÈ ESPRESSO SHAKERATO . . . €15.00", "CIOCCOLATA CALDA CON PANNA . . . €17.00", "BELLINI . . . €19.00". — [Quadri piazza menu PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0508/7356/5353/files/Menu_Piazza_Quadri_24_-_review_-_SINGOLE_d5773d5d-d9ed-43e8-8618-6cd8e0b05a23.pdf)
- **Pedrocchi price format (counter-example):** dotted leaders to a right-hand price with a decimal comma and no currency sign: "Espresso ..... 3 ,00", "Pedrocchi Coffee (7) ..... 6 ,00", "Marocchino Coffee (7) ..... 4 ,00". — [Pedrocchi menu PDF](https://caffepedrocchi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/past_en.pdf)
- Descriptive labels: Wansink and colleagues found descriptive menu labels raise purchase frequency and satisfaction (as summarised on Wikipedia's menu-engineering article); the often-quoted 27% sales lift is attributed inconsistently across sources, and Wansink's wider body of work has had retractions, so treat the number as weak. — [Wikipedia, Menu engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menu_engineering)
- Caffè Florian prices are printed in the menu including the live-music supplement: a September 2026 Il Fatto Alimentare piece reports a bill of 72 euro for a toast, a croissant and two spritz with a 7 euro music supplement, noting all prices and the surcharge were shown in the menu; it also cites a 60 euro breakfast and 11 euro mineral water (search snippet only). — [Il Fatto Alimentare](https://ilfattoalimentare.it/venezia-72-euro-toast-croissant-spritz-supplemento-musica-menu.html)
- Harry's Bar Venice Bellini is reported at 22 euro (search snippet only). — [Trainline guide](https://www.thetrainline.com/via/europe/italy/venice/visiting-harrys-bar)

### Inferences
- For 1835 the Prada pattern is the cleanest to copy: currency stated once (e.g. "AED" as a column head or in the footer), bare numerals, no leaders, right-aligned narrow column, no ".00".
- Flat pricing per category (all mignon one price, all aperitivi one price) is what lets Prada drop most price clutter and state a price once per group; it also removes price as the basis of choice within a group.
- Leader dots are not "un-luxurious" in Italy; they signal a traditional caffè storico listino. Whether to use them is a brand-tone choice (heritage gazette vs. fashion-house booklet), not a rule.

### Gaps
- I could not open the Cornell PDF itself (HTTP 405), so the 201-diner sample size and $5.55 figure rest on the Cornell Chronicle summary, not the paper's tables.
- I found no peer-reviewed evidence on leader dots, price-column alignment, or decoy items; these remain consultant lore.
- I found no evidence in any accessed luxury menu of banco vs tavolo dual pricing being printed; this Italian convention is unverified here.

## 4. Paper stocks and print finishes

### Takeaway
Documented hospitality projects use uncoated, tactile papers, most often Fedrigoni Old Mill and G.F Smith Colorplan in the 270–350 gsm range for cards and covers, with hot foil, blind embossing and Pantone/metallic offset; durable covers are leather or fabric-padded board. I found no production credits for any of the named Italian cafés.

### Cited Findings
- Hotel Collegio alla Querce, Florence (5-star, Auberge Resorts Collection): all stationery including menus on **Fedrigoni Old Mill Bianco** ("creamy white paper with a textured finish"); four-colour and Pantone offset, **hot foil stamping** for certain titles and details, **dry (blind) embossing**; rigid elements padded in green fabric. — [Atelier Bulk](https://www.atelierbulk.com/en/?p=6948)
- Unedo boutique hotel (Designmono), search snippet only: snack and cocktail menus on **Colorplan Pistachio and Cool Blue 270 gsm**; spirits menu interiors on **Colorplan Mist, Powder Green and Cool Blue 270 gsm**; wine catalogue interior on **Colorplan Pale Grey 120 gsm**; **Old Mill Bianco 190–350 gsm** elsewhere; daily menus on Apolo Ivory 120 gsm with **bronze hot foil**; one-colour **Pantone metallic** offset for sousplats and key folders; **pyrography** on natural leather menu covers; paper brands listed: G.F Smith, Sappi, Cordenons, Fedrigoni. — [Designmono, Unedo](https://oleander-carnation-g9bx.squarespace.com/projects/unedo-boutique-hotel-visual-identity-printing)
- 1905 restaurant, Mayfair (Designmono), search snippet only: Fedrigoni paper with **white hot foil** on the main menu, business cards and standee. — [Designmono, 1905](https://oleander-carnation-g9bx.squarespace.com/projects/visual-identity-printing-for-1905-restaurant-mayfair)
- Montreux Café identity (FPO, 2011), search snippet only: 420 gsm stock with copper foil-blocking of the logo. — [FPO, Montreux Café](https://underconsideration.com/fpo/archives/2011/04/montreux-cafe-identity-system.php)
- Meraki, London (Progress Packaging), search snippet only: outer menu covers foil-blocked in several colours; bill folders with a one-colour foiled deboss logo. — [Creativepool, Progress Packaging](https://creativepool.com/progresspkg/projects/meraki-menus-for-meraki-london/who-liked)
- 1901 restaurant for Hyatt (Fluid), search snippet only: sculpted embossing combined with foil blocking on a fine-dining menu. — [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/computer-arts/combine-special-finishes-9098999)
- Bar Gabbiano at Hotel Cipriani prints "100% recycled paper" on its menu. — [Bar Gabbiano menu PDF](https://pdfs.belmond.com/cip-gabbianobar-cocktails-eng.pdf)
- Hygiene constraint in Dubai: the Food Code says menu cards that come in contact with hands, food remnants and debris should be kept clean at all times. — [Dubai Food Code 2013 (English)](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/10/Food-Code-2013-English.pdf)

### Inferences
- A defensible spec for 1835 based on these precedents: wipeable or replaceable cover (leather, book-cloth over board, or a heavy duplexed uncoated board) with a blind-debossed or foil-stamped "1835"; inner pages on 120–170 gsm uncoated (Old Mill / Colorplan class) that can be reprinted digitally when prices change, as Unedo does (foil/leather cover + digitally printed interiors).
- Uncoated cotton-feel paper stains; the Dubai cleanliness clause argues for either a protective cover system or cheap, frequently replaced inserts rather than a single precious letterpressed card.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed what paper or finishing Marchesi, Cova, Florian, Gilli, Prada Caffè, Bulgari or Armani use for their menus.
- I found no documented menu project using Gmund papers, letterpress, or edge painting; these are plausible but uncited for menus specifically.
- Several findings above are search-snippet level from a Squarespace mirror of Designmono's site; the direct page returned 404 when fetched.

## 5. Structure and vocabulary of a traditional Italian caffetteria / pasticceria menu

### Takeaway
Real Italian listini group items under Caffetteria, Cioccolata, Tè e infusi, Pasticceria, snack/tramezzini, gelato and aperitivo, and name each coffee preparation individually. The classic names and regional specials below are sourced; a handful of everyday terms are listed under Gaps because I did not capture a citable definition.

### Cited Findings
**Section structure seen in real listini**
- Pedrocchi sections: Healthy (fruit, juices, yoghurt); Caffetteria Pedrocchi; Our Tea with Biscuits; Our Hot Chocolate; Pasticceria; Snacks… Any Time (toast, pizza, sandwich, focaccia, piadina, salted croissant, tramezzino). — [Pedrocchi menu PDF](https://caffepedrocchi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/past_en.pdf)
- Pedrocchi caffetteria list, in order: Pedrocchi Coffee (house signature), Espresso, Decaffeinated, Barley Coffee, American Coffee, Vienna Coffee, Marocchino, Ginseng Coffee, Shaken Coffee, Macchiato, Cappuccino, Soy Milk Cappuccino, Vienna Cappuccino, Latte macchiato, Milk & Mint, Irish coffee, plus "splash" (corretto) options. — [Pedrocchi menu PDF](https://caffepedrocchi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/past_en.pdf)
- Pedrocchi pasticceria list: Artisan Croissant; Mignon Biscuits (8 pz); Mignon Pastry (5 pz); Mignon; Macaron; Piece of Cake (ricotta and chocolate, Sacher, peach and amaretto…); Zabaione; Pedrocchi Tiramisù; Cheesecake. Tramezzini flavours: tuna and pickled onions; salmon; ham and mushrooms; vegetarian; sopressa with Asiago. — [Pedrocchi menu PDF](https://caffepedrocchi.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/past_en.pdf)
- Quadri "Caffè" list, in order: Caffè espresso; espresso macchiato; decaffeinato; caffè doppio; caffè americano ("espresso with hot water and milk on the side"); caffè in filtro; caffelatte; espresso con panna; cappuccino; cappuccino con latte di mandorla; Lì per lì (mini cappuccino from Trieste); orzo biologico; orzo con crema di latte; latte macchiato; caffè espresso shakerato; cioccolata calda (winter only); cioccolata calda con panna; Irish coffee. It states the blend: 100% Arabica roasted exclusively by Giamaica Caffè, Verona. — [Quadri piazza menu PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0508/7356/5353/files/Menu_Piazza_Quadri_24_-_review_-_SINGOLE_d5773d5d-d9ed-43e8-8618-6cd8e0b05a23.pdf)
- Quadri breakfast pastry naming distinguishes "Croissant nature / alla crema / al pistacchio / al cioccolato" from "Brioche all'italiana nature / all'albicocca"; other headings: Drinks, Acque minerali, Succhi di frutta, Spremute di agrumi (arancia, pompelmo), Limonata fresca, Tè e infusi, Cicchetti, Classic Spritz, Signature Spritz. — [Quadri piazza menu PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0508/7356/5353/files/Menu_Piazza_Quadri_24_-_review_-_SINGOLE_d5773d5d-d9ed-43e8-8618-6cd8e0b05a23.pdf)
- Marchesi 1824 bookable breakfasts (OpenTable, search snippet only): a "colazione dolce" with a tiered stand ("alzatina") of mini filled bomboloni, financiers and a selection of pasticceria mignon, plus a hot drink and a fresh spremuta; coffee and tea "rituals" served with handmade mignon. — [OpenTable, Marchesi 1824](https://www.opentable.com/r/marchesi-1824-milano)

**Classic drinks and items, with definitions**
- Caffè / espresso: in an Italian bar "un caffè" means a straight espresso, normally drunk standing at the bar, served with saucer and small spoon, sometimes with water and a small chocolate. Ristretto: a shorter espresso. Macchiato: espresso "stained" with milk. Cappuccino: espresso topped with foamed milk. Caffellatte: coffee and milk in similar quantities, in a larger cup. Caffè con panna: with whipped cream. Caffè d'orzo: barley "coffee". Caffè al ginseng: coffee with ginseng extract. — [Wikipedia, Coffee in Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee_in_Italy)
- Latte macchiato: "steamed milk stained with coffee" (Quadri's own gloss). — [Quadri piazza menu PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0508/7356/5353/files/Menu_Piazza_Quadri_24_-_review_-_SINGOLE_d5773d5d-d9ed-43e8-8618-6cd8e0b05a23.pdf)
- Marocchino: a layered shot of espresso, cocoa powder and foamed milk, sometimes with chocolate, served in a small glass; described by Gambero Rosso as born in Alessandria in the first half of the 1900s as an evolution of the Piedmontese bicerin (search snippet only). — [The Tour Guy](https://thetourguy.com/travel-blog/italian-coffee-drinks/); [Gambero Rosso](https://www.gamberorosso.it/?p=118670)
- Bicerin (Turin, Piedmont): layered hot espresso and drinking chocolate topped with cream, served in a glass, dating to the 18th century. — [The Tour Guy](https://thetourguy.com/travel-blog/italian-coffee-drinks/)
- Caffè shakerato: espresso shaken with ice in a cocktail shaker and strained, usually sweetened, served in a coupe; typically a warm-months drink. — [The Tour Guy](https://thetourguy.com/travel-blog/italian-coffee-drinks/); [Wikipedia, Coffee in Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee_in_Italy)
- Caffè leccese / caffè alla salentina (Lecce, Salento, Puglia): fresh espresso poured over ice with almond syrup (some sources say almond milk; sources disagree). — [Babbel Magazine](https://www.babbel.com/en/magazine/how-to-drink-italian-coffee)
- Caffè alla nocciola (Naples): espresso with hazelnut cream; no fixed recipe, described as espresso, hazelnut paste and sugar. — [Dissapore](https://www.dissapore.com/grande-notizia/i-caffe-regionali-ditalia-da-torino-a-napoli/); [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/alla-scoperta-caffe-gourmet-napoletani-AElwsH1G)
- Granita di caffè con panna (Sicily): coffee granita served in a glass with whipped cream and a warm "brioche col tuppo". — [Dissapore](https://www.dissapore.com/grande-notizia/i-caffe-regionali-ditalia-da-torino-a-napoli/)
- Affogato: a scoop of gelato "drowned" with espresso. Quadri's version: vanilla gelato, double espresso, whipped cream. — [Wikipedia, Coffee in Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee_in_Italy); [Quadri piazza menu PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0508/7356/5353/files/Menu_Piazza_Quadri_24_-_review_-_SINGOLE_d5773d5d-d9ed-43e8-8618-6cd8e0b05a23.pdf)
- Gran caffè (Sant'Eustachio, Rome): house speciality from a secret blend, served in a slightly larger cup, pre-sweetened with a light crema; ask "amaro" for no sugar (search snippet only). — [Time Out Rome](https://www.timeout.com/rome/restaurants/caffe-santeustachio)
- Tramezzino: soft crustless white-bread triangle sandwich; origin attributed to Caffè Mulassano, Turin, named by D'Annunzio in 1926; the classic filling is butter and anchovy, and Mulassano offers over thirty, including lobster and truffle. — [Gambero Rosso](https://www.gamberorosso.it/notizie/rubriche/storie/tramezzino-storia-torino/); [Turismo Torino](https://www.turismotorino.org/en/your-trip/where-to-eat/caffe-mulassano)
- Bellini: fresh white peach and prosecco, invented by Giuseppe Cipriani in 1948; Hotel Cipriani serves it only in season. — [Bar Gabbiano menu PDF](https://pdfs.belmond.com/cip-gabbianobar-cocktails-eng.pdf)
- Aperitivo classics as worded at Prada: Spritz Aperol (Aperol, prosecco, soda); Spritz Campari; Negroni (Campari, vermouth, gin). Soft drinks include Chinotto Lurisia. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- Gelato listing convention at Prada: one price for "Selection of three flavours", then flavours in two columns (chocolate, stracciatella, cream, hazelnut, tiramisù, pistachio; fruit sorbet-style flavours flagged vegan). — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)

### Inferences
- Correct Italian section titles for 1835, consistent with the listini above: "Caffetteria", "Cioccolata", "Tè e Infusi", "Pasticceria", "Tramezzini" (or "Salato"), "Gelato", "Spremute e Succhi", "Aperitivo".
- Every prestige café lists a named house coffee first (Pedrocchi Coffee, Sant'Eustachio's Gran Caffè, Quadri's exclusive blend statement). A signature "Caffè 1835" at the head of the Caffetteria section follows that pattern.
- Stating the roaster and blend in one line under the section title (Quadri) is a low-cost credibility signal.

### Gaps
- Not captured from a citable source in this session (widely known, but verify before print): lungo, caffè corretto, cornetto vs brioche regional usage, cioccolata calda "in tazza" style, mignon / monoporzione / torta terminology, spremuta vs succo, and Italian non-alcoholic aperitivi (e.g. Crodino, Sanbittèr, "analcolico della casa").

## 7. Practical points for Dubai

### Takeaway
Dubai's Food Code requires menus in both Arabic and English with allergens (and any non-halal items) clearly identified; calorie labelling is governed by a Dubai Municipality guide that is voluntary unless the venue opts in or makes nutrition/health claims; displayed prices must include VAT, and venues print a one-line fee statement. Alcohol-free aperitivo is handled as its own titled section priced just below the alcoholic list.

### Cited Findings
**Language**
- Dubai Food Code s.8.3(a): "Food service establishments such as restaurants and cafeteria shall have product menu printed both in Arabic and English. Foods with allergens or Non-Halal ingredients shall be clearly identified in the menu." — [Dubai Food Code 2013 (English)](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/10/Food-Code-2013-English.pdf)
- Bilingual layout guidance (industry sources): an Arabic layout should be mirrored, not just translated, with prices, navigation and descriptions on the opposite side from the LTR version; a Saudi coffee-shop design guide warns against layouts where prices sit in the middle with text at the edges; decide deliberately whether Arabic pages use Arabic-Indic or Western numerals; allow extra room for translated copy. — [Pickcel](https://www.pickcel.com/blog/multilingual-digital-menu-board/); [Saudi Coffee Crafters](https://saudicoffeecrafters.com/?p=15533); [Globalization Partners](https://www.globalizationpartners.com/2026/04/02/restaurant-menu-translation/)
- Arabic and Latin differ in apparent size; Arabic can look smaller than Latin at the same point size, and the Arabic typeface library is smaller, so pairing needs care. — [Khajeh thesis, University of Hertfordshire](https://uhra.herts.ac.uk/id/eprint/25991/1/14188519%20KHAJEH%20Sahar%20Final%20Submission%20October%202024.pdf)

**Allergens and halal**
- Food Code s.3.4.3: the following must be declared on the label or menu whenever present, however small the amount: crustaceans; peanuts; soybeans; tree nuts; sesame seeds; fish; egg; milk; gluten and cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, oats, barley, spelt). For food prepared for immediate consumption, allergen information "must either be displayed on, or provided to the purchaser upon request". A venue offering allergen-free food should have a competent person on every shift. — [Dubai Food Code 2013 (English)](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/10/Food-Code-2013-English.pdf)
- Food Code s.8.7.4: a restaurant serving non-halal food must say so clearly on the first page of the menu card and identify each such item, listed apart from other foods. — [Dubai Food Code 2013 (English)](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/10/Food-Code-2013-English.pdf)
- In June 2023 Dubai Municipality officials said a "Food Code 2.0" identifying 14 allergens and requiring clear menu labelling was in the final stages of approval; the article gives no effective date. — [Khaleej Times, 7 June 2023](https://khaleejtimes.com/lifestyle/food/dubai-got-food-allergies-new-rules-to-require-restaurants-to-display-more-details-on-menus)

**Calories**
- Dubai Municipality's calorie guide (updated 22 Nov 2020) says Section 1 requirements "are not mandatory unless the establishments are certified under the scheme"; Section 2 is mandatory for any venue that makes calorie/nutrient declarations or "healthy" claims. — [DM Guide for Calorie Declaration](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/04/Guide-for-Calorie-Declaration-in-Food-Establishments-v30-22-Nov-2020.pdf)
- If calories are declared: show the number followed by "Calories" or "Cal", adjacent to the item name or price, in a font no smaller than the name or price; a column format may use "Calories"/"Cal" as the column head; 20% maximum variance; variable items as "Cal 400/550" (two options) or a range. Alcoholic beverages, daily specials and temporary items (under 60 days a year) are exempt. — [DM Guide for Calorie Declaration](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/04/Guide-for-Calorie-Declaration-in-Food-Establishments-v30-22-Nov-2020.pdf)
- Required statements when declaring: "2000 calories a day is used for general nutrition advice for youth and adults (ages 13 and older) while 1500 calories for children (ages 4 to 12), but individual needs vary." and "Additional nutrition information is available upon request", on the first menu page. The word "healthy" may only be used via the municipality's Eat Healthy, Live Healthy certification. — [DM Guide for Calorie Declaration](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/04/Guide-for-Calorie-Declaration-in-Food-Establishments-v30-22-Nov-2020.pdf)
- Conflict: 2019 press reported calorie display as compulsory in phases (outlets with more than five branches first, hotels later), whereas the 2020 guide frames it as opt-in. — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-municipality-asks-food-establishments-to-declare-calorie-content-on-menu); contradicted by [DM Guide for Calorie Declaration](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/04/Guide-for-Calorie-Declaration-in-Food-Establishments-v30-22-Nov-2020.pdf)
- Design precedent for inline calories on a luxury menu: Prada prints them in running text after the item name ("Hot chocolate 396 kcal"). — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)

**Prices, VAT and fees**
- The UAE Federal Tax Authority requires displayed prices to be inclusive of VAT; press reports of the penalty differ (Dh15,000 in the 2018 FTA statement, Dh5,000 in a later comment). — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/hszr6ptm-fta-businesses-must-display-vat-inclusive-prices); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/your-money/taxation/businesses-must-display-prices-inclusive-of-vat-1.2187508)
- Typical Dubai footer wording: "All prices are in UAE Dirhams inclusive of 5% VAT, 7% Municipality Fees and 10% Service Charge" (Legoland Dubai); "All prices are in UAE Dirhams (AED) and inclusive of VAT, Municipality Fee, and Service Charge" (voco Dubai); Four Seasons DIFC's Penrose Lounge words it as inclusive of 10% service charge and 5% VAT and "subject to 7% Municipality fee" (search snippets only). — [Legoland Dubai menu](https://www.legoland.com/dubai/media/ymbnlzql/skyline-menu-october-2026.pdf); [Four Seasons DIFC](https://fourseasons.com/dubaidifc/dining/lounges/penrose_lounge/afternoon-tea)

**Alcohol-free aperitivo**
- Prada Caffè gives non-alcoholic cocktails their own titled block, "NO ALCOHOL", directly under the signature list, each with a coined name and an ingredient line, priced 17 against 18–20 for alcoholic drinks: Bosco Estivo (Everleaf Forest, orange, cranberry, strawberry syrup, ginger ale); Espresso Minthini (Lavazza espresso, hazelnut syrup, green mint syrup); Hammam Rosé (hibiscus infusion, rose and clove syrup, grapefruit soda); Green Ume (lime cordial, green tea, peach and jasmine, salted syrup). Also "Sparkling tea" (Saicho Darjeeling, Jasmine, Hojicha) at 15. — [Prada Caffè menu PDF](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradanux/pradasphere/2024/special-projects/07/prada_caffe/Menu_Prada_Caffe.pdf)
- Bar Gabbiano lists "MOCKTAILS" at 24 against 30–32 for cocktails, including a "Virgin Bellini" (fresh peach, soda water) and "Riviera" built on Tanqueray 0. — [Bar Gabbiano menu PDF](https://pdfs.belmond.com/cip-gabbianobar-cocktails-eng.pdf)
- In Dubai, Beefbar lists a 0% Negroni and a spritz made with Lyre's Italian Spritz, labelled as a non-alcoholic version of the Italian classic; TOTO Dubai has a separate mocktail menu with Lyre's-based spritz and aperitivo serves (search snippets only). — [Beefbar Dubai bar menu](https://beefbar.com/menus/dubai/bar.pdf); [TOTO Dubai mocktails](https://totorestaurants.com/dubai/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/TOTO-Dubai-Mocktails.pdf)

### Inferences
- Because Arabic and English are both mandatory, the cleanest luxury solution is a mirrored booklet (English reading from one cover, Arabic from the other, meeting in the middle) or facing pages with a shared central price column avoided; a single page with Latin left / Arabic right and prices in the gutter is the layout the regional guide warns against.
- Since Prada's "kcal" inline treatment and DM's rule ("Cal" adjacent to name or price, same size or larger) differ, a Dubai menu that opts in cannot set calories as tiny grey footnotes; plan the typographic hierarchy for a same-size calorie figure or stay out of the scheme and make no nutrition claims.
- A compliant, discreet footer for 1835 would combine: allergen key (the nine Food Code groups, or 14 if Food Code 2.0 is in force), "Prices in AED, inclusive of 5% VAT [and applicable fees]", and the calorie statements only if calories are shown.
- For an unlicensed café, the aperitivo section can follow Prada/Gabbiano: titled as its own ritual with coined Italian names and ingredient lines, priced close to but below a cocktail, built on bitter/botanical 0% bases rather than juices.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Food Code 2.0 (14 allergens) has been formally issued or is in force as of November 2026; only the 2013 code text was verified. Confirm with Dubai Municipality.
- The current legal status of calorie labelling (mandatory vs opt-in) in 2026 is unresolved between the 2019 press and the 2020 guide; I found no newer official document.
- Whether a 7% municipality fee and 10% service charge apply to a standalone café (as opposed to a hotel outlet) was not established; the examples found are hotels and attractions.
- No source addressed Dubai alcohol licensing for cafés or labelling rules for 0.0% drinks.
- No authoritative style guide for bilingual Arabic/English printed menus was found; the layout points come from vendor blogs plus one academic thesis.
- The Bulgari Resort Dubai La Spiaggia beverage menu exists online but was not read (Bulgari PDFs returned 403). — [Bulgari Resort Dubai PDF link](https://www.bulgarihotels.com/dam/jcr:24453071-ac20-4d3c-a817-22efd9cd2a01/Bvlgari%20Resort%20Dubai_La%20Spiaggia_Beverage%20Menu.pdf)
