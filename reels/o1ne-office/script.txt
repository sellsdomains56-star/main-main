Before you buy an office in Dubai, look at what's developing in Motor City. O1NE District by AVENEW and KORA sits next to Dubai Autodrome. Its master plan includes six commercial towers, retail, healthcare facilities and landscaped public spaces. DAWN is the first office tower, with direct mall connectivity, sky gardens, outdoor workspaces and rooftop dining planned. For a company choosing its next office, those details matter. Where will staff park? How will clients arrive? Can people reach cafes and services without driving somewhere else? That makes this district worth comparing with established office locations. But don't take a rent achieved in Dubai Hills or Marina and assume you'll achieve it in Motor City. The tenants, budgets and competing supply are different. Check the allocated parking, usable office area, fit-out costs, service charges and delivery schedule. Then compare the purchase price with realistic rental evidence.
