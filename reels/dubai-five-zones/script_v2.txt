Five Dubai zones - script v2 (geography corrected against the real road lines; changes marked in the notes below)

Stop investing in Dubai without understanding these five zones, or you could end up buying in the wrong location.

Zone 1, Sheikh Zayed Road and the coastline: Dubai Marina, JBR, Palm Jumeirah, Downtown, and Business Bay. Prime locations, luxury properties, and strong rental demand. Prices range from 2,000 to 5,000 AED per square foot.

Zone 2, between E11 and E311: Dubai Hills, JVC, Arjan, and Al Barsha South. Family communities with stable rental demand and growth potential. Prices range from 1,200 to 1,800 AED per square foot.

Zone 3, between E311 and E611: Arabian Ranches 3, Motor City, Tilal Al Ghaf, and Dubai Land. More villas, townhouses, and family living. Prices range from 900 to 1,400 AED per square foot.

Zone 4, the affordable frontier: Majan, Liwan, and beyond E611, The Valley and DAMAC Hills 2. Prices range from 700 to 1,100 AED per square foot. Lower entry prices, but you're investing in future infrastructure and growth.

Zone 5, Dubai South and the airport corridor: Expo City, Al Maktoum International Airport, and the wider Palm Jebel Ali corridor. Major infrastructure investment and long-term development potential. Selected properties range from 1,100 to 1,500 AED per square foot.
