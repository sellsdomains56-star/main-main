How is Dubai's royal family connected to the developers building the city? Start with Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed. He chairs ICD, the Investment Corporation of Dubai, which manages a major portfolio of government investments. ICD owns Ithra Dubai, the developer behind One Za'abeel. It also has a stake in Dubai Islamic Bank, which is a major shareholder in Deyaar. Then there's Sheikh Maktoum bin Mohammed. He chairs Dubai Real Estate Corporation, which owns and manages government properties and land. Wasl is its real estate operating arm, developing and managing homes, offices, hotels, and leisure destinations. Next is Dubai Holding, chaired by Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed. It's government-owned, and its portfolio brings together Nakheel, Meraas, Dubai Properties, and Meydan. In May {2026,|twenty twenty six} Dubai Holding also acquired ICD's stake in Emaar, taking its total shareholding to {29.73%|twenty nine point seven three percent} and making it Emaar's largest shareholder. Sheikh Ahmed also chairs Dubai Aviation City Corporation, the government entity behind Dubai South, and Expo City Dubai Authority. These are the connections. Members of Dubai's royal family chair major government entities, and those entities own businesses, land, and stakes in developers across the city. Chairing the organization and personally owning its assets are two different things.
