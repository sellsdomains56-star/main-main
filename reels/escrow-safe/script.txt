Before you pay for an off-plan property in Dubai, check where your money is going. Dubai's escrow framework requires developers selling off-plan to use a project account with an approved bank or financial institution, subject to the applicable rules. Buyer payments go into that account for the project's development. The funds cannot be seized to satisfy the developer's creditors. The law also requires a {5%|five percent} retention after the completion certificate, with release one year after units are registered in purchasers' names. If a developer fails to complete, the law provides procedures aimed at completion or returning payments. That does not mean an immediate or guaranteed full refund in every case. Escrow is an important protection. It doesn't remove construction delays, market risk, or the need to read your contract before paying.
