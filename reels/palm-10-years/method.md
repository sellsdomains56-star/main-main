# Palm Jumeirah villa price per sq ft, 2016-2026 - method and caveats

Source: Dubai Land Department sales transactions as published through Bayut's "Sale Transactions for Villas in Palm Jumeirah" (powered by Tru View), pulled 15 Oct 2026 with the 120-month window: 1,406 villa sales, 11 Oct 2016 to 13 Oct 2026. Of these 1,143 have a built-up area and are used (the 263 without area, mostly first sales of off-plan homes, are excluded).
Metric: transaction price / built-up area, converted to sq ft (1 sqm = 10.7639 sq ft). Per calendar year: the MEDIAN (robust against a few wrong areas). 2016 = from 11 Oct only (n=24); 2026 = to 13 Oct (n=58).
Note: Bayut's own headline "average price per sqm" divides by PLOT area, so it reads lower (about AED 5,300/sq ft for the last 12 months); built-up area is how villa prices per sq ft are usually quoted.

Derived: 2016->2026 x3.34 (+234%), compound about 12.8% a year; 2016->2019 -11.5%; 2020->2023 x2.89; 2020->2026 x4.16. Cross-check: classic frond villas only (Garden Homes + Signature Villas, resales): 2,550 -> 8,610 = x3.38.

Caveats to keep in the video: (1) median of what sold, not a repeat-sales index - newer, bigger, higher-priced product (Palma Residences, Palm Crown) lifts it; (2) 2016 baseline is only Oct-Dec and 2026 is year-to-date; (3) past performance is not a promise; (4) DLD data can contain wrong areas - the median limits the effect, but check a few transactions before posting.
