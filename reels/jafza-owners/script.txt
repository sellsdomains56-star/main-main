Which member of Dubai's royal family is connected to Jebel Ali Free Zone? Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum. He chairs Dubai World, the government-owned group at the top of its ownership chain. Here's how it connects. Dubai World owns Port and Free Zone World. That company owns DP World, and JAFZA operates within the DP World group. So Sheikh Ahmed's connection is through his chairmanship of Dubai World. The ultimate owner is the Government of Dubai. The history goes back to {1980,|nineteen eighty} when a law established the free zone. In {1985,|nineteen eighty five} another decree created its governing authority. Then, in {2020,|twenty twenty} Port and Free Zone World bought out DP World's public shareholders and took the company off Nasdaq Dubai. There's also outside investment. In {2022,|twenty twenty two} Canadian investor CDPQ acquired a minority interest in a structure covering JAFZA, Jebel Ali Port, and National Industries Park. DP World retained control.
