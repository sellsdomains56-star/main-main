# Hooks for the Aldar / Silicon Oasis video (all truthful - no false claim is used to get the click)

Nobody can guarantee a video goes viral. What raises the odds: a hook that creates a question in the first 2 seconds, a pattern change every ~3 s (the map beats do this), an open loop that is paid off mid-video, a save-worthy takeaway, and a last line that loops back to the first. Test 3 hooks on the same body and double down on the winner (check 3-second hold and shares after 24 h).

## 12 hooks (spoken line | on-screen text, max 6 words)
Curiosity gap
1. "Why would Aldar build a townhouse community on top of a metro line that isn't open until twenty twenty-nine?" | ON TOP OF A METRO THAT DOESN'T EXIST
2. "Aldar's CEO let one detail slip about its next Dubai community. Here it is." | THE ONE DETAIL HE LET SLIP
3. "I can't tell you the name. I can't tell you the price. But I can tell you what Aldar did say." | NO NAME. NO PRICE. ONE CLUE.
4. "Aldar is about to launch in Dubai. Watch this before they do." | BEFORE ALDAR LAUNCHES
Contrarian
5. "Dubai home sales fell forty-seven percent last quarter. So why is Abu Dhabi's biggest developer launching more?" | SALES -47%. ALDAR LAUNCHES ANYWAY.
6. "Everyone's waiting. Aldar isn't." | EVERYONE WAITS. ALDAR DOESN'T.
Insider / authority
7. "Here's what Aldar's CEO told The National this week, in forty seconds." | WHAT ALDAR'S CEO JUST SAID
8. "Three questions I'd ask before buying any new Dubai townhouse." | 3 QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU BUY
Pattern interrupt (map)
9. (map zooms onto Silicon Oasis, a pulsing "?" appears) "This is the spot Aldar picked. And there's a reason." | WHY HERE?
10. "A metro line. A townhouse community. A launch date. Put those three together." | PUT THESE 3 TOGETHER
Social-proof / track record
11. "Aldar will hand over more than three thousand homes this year. Its biggest ever. Now it's coming to Dubai." | ITS BIGGEST YEAR EVER
Direct
12. "If you're looking at a townhouse in Dubai, stop scrolling." | TOWNHOUSE BUYERS: WATCH

Best three to test: 1, 5, 3.

## Retention structure (master script `speaker_master.txt`)
0-3 s hook + map zoom | 3-12 s the reveal (what Aldar said) | 12-20 s open loop paid off: "sits above the Blue Line extension" | 20-30 s Blue Line facts (route drawn on the map) | 30-40 s "my read" + balance (47% drop, no promise) | 40-48 s CTA with a keyword, then the loop-back line that returns to the hook.
