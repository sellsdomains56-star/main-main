Aldar, one of Dubai's tier-one developers, is about to launch a new community near Silicon Oasis. I went through the sales data to see what its earlier launches suggest. Aldar has launched three communities in Dubai so far: Haven, Athlon and The Wilds. Together they have registered almost eighteen billion dirhams of sales. On price, each launch was higher than the one before: about one thousand two hundred and thirty dirhams per square foot at Haven, one thousand three hundred and sixty at Athlon, one thousand eight hundred and seventy-five at The Wilds. Roughly eleven percent up, then thirty-eight. Emaar townhouses resell at about one thousand four hundred dirhams per square foot. Haven and Athlon launched at or below that; The Wilds well above. My estimate for the new community is level with Emaar, or a little above. After launch, resale prices at Haven and Athlon grew three to four percent last year, while Emaar and Damac communities grew eleven to thirteen. Aldar hasn't handed over a home in Dubai yet; the first, at Haven, is due in twenty twenty-seven. So I'd estimate about three point two to three point eight million dirhams for a three-bedroom: as low as one thousand two hundred and fifty per square foot if Aldar prices cautiously, around one thousand nine hundred if it prices like The Wilds.
