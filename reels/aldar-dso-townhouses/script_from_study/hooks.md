# Hooks: analytic, calm, no promises, no urgency, no "sold out in days". They say what is happening and what the data will be used for; the study is the background after the first sentence.

Default (line 1 of every script):
1. "Aldar, one of Dubai's tier-one developers, is about to launch a new community near Silicon Oasis. I went through the sales data to see what its earlier launches suggest."

Alternatives (swap line 1 only; everything after still works):
2. "A new Aldar community is planned near Dubai Silicon Oasis. It hasn't been priced yet, so I looked at how its last three launches were priced."
3. "Tier-one developers keep launching new communities in Dubai. Here's what the sales records show for Aldar's, before its next one."
4. "What do Aldar's first three Dubai launches tell us about its fourth?"

Rules: no claim about how much buyers buy or how fast things sell in the hook; every number in the video is in the study; never a project name or a price as Aldar's; record the hook as its own take so it can be swapped.
