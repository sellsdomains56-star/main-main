# Hooks (first 3-4 seconds). None of them starts on the study or on "I worked it out": they speak to the viewer, the study only appears as the background after the hook.

Default (line 1 of the scripts):
1. "New community. New community. New community. Aldar keeps launching them in Dubai, and people buy a lot, in days. So what happens to the buyers after?" (say the three \"new community\" fast, one per beat, like three punches; ~11 s)
   Short form of the same idea: "Another Aldar community. And another. And another. All sold out in days. What happens to the buyers after?"

Previous default (still works as an alternative): "Every Aldar launch in Dubai sells out in days. So what happens to the buyers after?"

Alternatives (swap line 1 only; every line after still works):
2. "Aldar is about to launch a new townhouse community in Dubai. Here's what nobody tells you before launch day."
3. "Before you queue for Aldar's next Dubai launch, look at what happened to the last three."
4. "What will Aldar's next Dubai townhouse cost? Nobody knows yet. But there's a way to get close."
5. "Dubai's biggest launches sell out in 48 hours. Nobody checks what the resale looks like six months later."

Rules: every number in a hook is in the study (page refs in beats.md); never a project name or a price as Aldar's; record the hook as its own take so it can be swapped.
