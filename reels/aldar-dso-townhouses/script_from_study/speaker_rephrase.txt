A new community is set to launch right next to Dubai Silicon Oasis. If Aldar prices it like its first two Dubai launches, a three-bedroom townhouse could come in below about one thousand four hundred dirhams per square foot. My estimate runs from one thousand four hundred to one thousand six hundred and fifty, with a lower case of around one thousand two hundred and fifty.

For context, Haven launched about twelve percent below comparable resale prices, and Athlon about two percent below, while The Wilds launched above them. Resales at Haven and Athlon are now above their launch prices, by about nine percent and three percent, and the top tenth of resales were around twenty percent or more above. That happened within the first couple of years, before the first handover in twenty twenty-seven.

Aldar has registered almost eighteen billion dirhams of sales across three Dubai launches, so demand at launch is well documented. Its earlier communities were released in phases, so if it follows the same pattern, this first release would be the first of several.

On location, the data points to two supports: the planned Blue Line, targeted to open in September twenty twenty-nine, and the fact that no townhouse sale has been recorded in Silicon Oasis in five years, so this would be new supply there.
