Aldar, one of Dubai's tier-one developers, is about to launch a new community near Silicon Oasis. It hasn't been priced, so I looked at what its last three launches suggest.

Each launch was priced higher than the one before, from about three hundred and thirty to five hundred and ten dollars per square foot. My estimate for the new one is three hundred and eighty to four hundred and fifty dollars. That's an estimate, not an Aldar figure.

After launch, resale prices at Haven and Athlon grew three to four percent last year, against eleven to thirteen at Emaar and Damac communities.
