Three large areas of land along Dubai's Sheikh Zayed Road corridor worth watching, and one already has a confirmed master plan. First, the canal area on the mainland near Palm Jebel Ali. Nakheel commissioned excavation works here back in {2007|two thousand seven} as part of the wider Dubai Waterfront project. The location is worth following as Palm Jebel Ali develops, but a new marina-style community and its launch date still need official confirmation. Second, the land around Badrah in Hessyan First. It sits within the wider Jebel Ali area, close to established employment centres and major waterfront development. Watch for an official announcement before treating talk of a new Dubai Holding community as a confirmed launch. Third, the land around Jebel Ali Racecourse. A.R.M. Holding has already announced a five-square-kilometre master plan designed with Bjarke Ingels Group. The plans include eight mixed-use neighbourhoods, homes, public spaces, and a large central park, with development planned in phases. These sites could introduce substantial new communities into the corridor. The launch prices, infrastructure, and delivery schedules will determine which opportunities are worth considering.
