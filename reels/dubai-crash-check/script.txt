Someone tells you Dubai property has crashed. Ask them three things: which area, which property type, and compared with when? Reporting based on Dubai Land Department records puts total transactions for January to September {2026|twenty twenty six} at approximately {AED 574 billion.|AED five hundred seventy four billion} Sales accounted for around {AED 379 billion|AED three hundred seventy nine billion} of that total. Mortgages and gifts are separate categories. Those figures show substantial market activity. They don't prove every property increased in value. A market can record large transaction totals while certain owners cut prices, certain buildings face weaker rents, and other locations remain competitive. The same applies to off-plan sales. A high share of off-plan transactions tells you what buyers are purchasing. It doesn't guarantee their eventual returns. So before calling the whole market a crash or a guaranteed opportunity, compare completed sales, rental evidence, and upcoming supply in the exact location.
