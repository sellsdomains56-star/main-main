## What I could not verify / what to keep straight
- He said **"ideal"**, not "best", and **"invest in Dubai and its infrastructure"**, not "Dubai and the UAE". The script keeps his words; do not put "UAE" in his mouth.
- The Dh2.1 trillion is a **pipeline of active projects** (in the Media Office's words "portfolio of ongoing projects"), not money already spent or contracts already awarded. Real estate Dh1.1 trillion is allocated "through 2030".
- The Dh100 billion covers roads **plus** the Blue and Gold lines together; do not say it is the Blue Line's budget. The Blue Line opening date is the RTA's **target**.
- The user first said "1 trillion" for real estate; the figure is Dh1.1 trillion, and "more than half" (52%) is the safe way to say it.
- 350,000 homes: **planned**, no date in the sources. Do not say "by 2030".
- The quote is about investing in Dubai and its infrastructure in general, not property returns. No return, price-rise or yield claim is made. The balance lines ("pipeline, not a guarantee", "more supply") are the speaker's own view, not his.
- Check UAE/Dubai property-advertising rules (permit numbers, regulator wording, disclaimers) before posting.
