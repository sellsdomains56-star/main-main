## Facts to check before posting
- Rockefeller tower: 87 storeys (400 m) and five amenity floors are official (LMD; sold through Devmark; completion Q2 2031). **332 offices appears only on a broker listing** (Engel & Völkers, which also says 372 m); shown as such.
- Berkeley Tower is Ellington Properties with Al Hamra Group (the voice says "Ellington and Al Hamra").
- AHS: the AED 1.1bn Shangri-La purchase was announced 11 Jun 2026 (hotel unchanged then); the Grade A office conversion on 27 Oct 2026 (G+50, Gensler; handover 30 Jun 2028 per the project site).
- Names: DAWN at O1NE District (letter O, digit 1) by AVENEW Development and KORA Properties (the voice says "DA Dawn" / "K-Aura"). "Six commercial towers" is the developer's phrase; the mall is a future phase.
- The Yards Plaza: BEYOND is an OMNIYAT Group brand; launched 7 Oct 2026, completion Q3 2029. Not the same as the nearby "272 residences" at Arancia Yards.
- Renders are the developers' marketing images; site pins are approximate (Rockefeller ±600 m, DAWN and Yards Plaza ±1 km).
