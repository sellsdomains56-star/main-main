Dubai's population reached around {4.8|four point eight} million in September. At the end of {2025|twenty twenty five}, it was {4.58|four point five eight} million, after an annual increase of approximately {332,000|three hundred thirty two thousand} people. The counting methodology has also been updated, so these figures shouldn't all be treated as people who just arrived. But a larger population still means more demand for housing, transport, schools, and healthcare. For property investors, you need to know where those households want to live and what they can afford. A family looking for a townhouse near a school isn't necessarily a tenant for a luxury studio on the other side of Dubai. Population growth supports demand. Whether your property benefits depends on its location, price, and the homes competing with it. The city is growing. Can housing and infrastructure keep pace? And where will the pressure be greatest?
