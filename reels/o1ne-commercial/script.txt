Before you buy a commercial property in Dubai, look at what's coming to Motor City. O1NE District by AVENEW and KORA sits next to Dubai Autodrome. The master plan includes six commercial towers, retail, healthcare facilities and landscaped public spaces. DAWN is the first office tower in the district, with direct mall connectivity, sky gardens, outdoor workspaces and rooftop dining planned. For a business choosing an office, the working day matters. How easily can staff arrive? Where do clients park? Can people reach cafes, shops and services without getting back in the car? That's what makes a connected business district worth comparing with established office locations. But don't take a rent achieved in Dubai Hills or Marina and apply it straight to Motor City. Different locations attract different tenants and budgets. Before buying, check your office's allocated parking, usable area, fit-out requirements, service charges and handover terms. Then compare the total cost with realistic rental evidence from the surrounding market. O1NE District deserves a place on your shortlist. The right unit still needs to justify its price.
